A simple, fast footy score app for Australian rules football.
Track goals and behinds in real time, visualise match flow, and save every game.

Designed for goal umpires, team managers, coaches, parents, and footy fans who want an easy way to keep score on game day.

FEATURES

LIVE SCORING
• Real-time tracking of goals and behinds during matches
• Quarter-by-quarter scoring with running totals
• Share in-progress game results with friends and team social media groups

SCORE VISUALISATION
• Interactive score worm chart showing match momentum
• Quarter-by-quarter breakdown and statistics
• Running differentials to see how the game unfolds

TEAM MANAGEMENT
• Create and manage footy teams with logos and branding
• Mark favourite teams for quick access
• Integrated maps to help navigate to away team games and venues

GAME HISTORY
• Save and review completed matches
• Share scores and statistics with others
